When shopping for a flagship smartphone in Kenya, the choice almost always comes down to Apple's iPhone 15 Pro Max and Samsung's Galaxy S24 Ultra. Both devices command premium prices in the Nairobi market (KSH 140,000 – KSH 195,000), so making an informed choice before hitting Add to Cart is essential.

Key Differences to Consider in Kenya

1. Resale Value & Local Demand: Apple iPhones retain significantly higher second-hand value in Nairobi hubs like Luthuli Avenue and Westlands compared to Android flagships.

2. Battery & Charging: The S24 Ultra charges faster via USB-C (45W) which is handy during travel across Kenya, whereas the iPhone 15 Pro Max boasts incredible battery optimization for heavy social media use.

3. Warranty & Verified Sellers: Always ensure you purchase from a Verified Seller on SokoHub to guarantee genuine Apple or Samsung international warranty.
