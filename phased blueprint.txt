Phase 1: Build an MVP (1–2 months)

The goal is to get real users.

Features
User registration and login
Seller registration
Seller dashboard
Add/Edit/Delete products
Categories (Cars, Rentals, Electronics, Services, etc.)
Product search
Product filters
Product details page
Contact seller (WhatsApp, phone, or chat)
Admin dashboard
Approve or reject listings

No online payments yet.

At this stage, buyers contact sellers directly, just like Jiji.

Phase 2: Start earning (2–4 weeks)

Once people are using the platform, add monetization.

Add:
Featured ads
Premium seller accounts
Paid listings
Seller analytics
Banner advertising

Now you can earn before implementing full e-commerce.

Phase 3: Marketplace

This is when your platform becomes more like Amazon.

Add:

Shopping cart
Checkout
M-Pesa payments
Order tracking
Commission system
Seller payouts

Example:

Customer pays KSh 5,000.
Your platform keeps 8%.
Seller receives KSh 4,600.
Phase 4: Scale

Once the website is stable:

Android app
iPhone app
In-app chat
Push notifications
Reviews and ratings
AI recommendations
Delivery integration
